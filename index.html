/ COMPANY PROFILE

Technology built around people, not buzzwords.

Stratos Ed Tech Systems is a student-led technology venture developing practical digital solutions for schools and businesses. We care about useful interfaces, reliable systems and work that addresses a real need.

/ THE IDEA

Make the complex work simply.

Our focus is education technology and business technology: public websites, learning systems, applications, technical integrations and upkeep. A project can be a new build or a repair that makes an existing system usable again.

We aim for clear scope, accessible digital experiences and a straightforward route from enquiry to delivery. The current service prices are published rather than hidden.

/ WHAT DRIVES THE WORK

Practical by design.

01 / CLARITY

Understand first.

Identify the workflow and the people using it before deciding what to build.

02 / UTILITY

Build for use.

Websites and applications should help someone complete a real task.

03 / CONTINUITY

Keep it working.

Deployment and support matter alongside development. Maintenance is listed separately.

/ OPERATING PRINCIPLE

Problem → solution → build → deploy → support.

Stratos is not limited to websites. We work across the digital tools that help schools and businesses operate.
